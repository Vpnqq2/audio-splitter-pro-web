About Audio Splitter Pro

Audio Splitter Pro separates a song into vocals, drums, bass and other instruments using an AI model that runs on your own device.

Why we built it

Most online vocal removers ask you to upload your music to a server, wait in a queue and sometimes pay. We wanted something simpler: open a page, pick a song, and let your own computer do the work. Your files stay with you.

What you can do

  • Split a song into four tracks: vocals, drums, bass and other.
  • Create a karaoke version in one tap.
  • Mix the tracks with volume, mute and solo controls.
  • Export a WAV file, or a ZIP with several tracks.

The technology

The site uses a Hybrid Transformer Demucs model converted to ONNX, executed in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web. You can read how this works in our guide to AI separation.

The Android app

The same idea is available as an Android app with MP3 export, voice recording over your music and a library of your songs. Get it on Google Play.
