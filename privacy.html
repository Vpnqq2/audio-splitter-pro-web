Privacy

Audio Splitter Pro for the web processes your music on your own device, inside your browser. The songs you select and the tracks created are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we have no account system and no database.

What is stored on your device

The AI model (about 165 MB) is saved in your browser's storage after the first download so it does not need to be downloaded again. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Third-party requests

To work, the page loads the ONNX Runtime Web library from the jsDelivr content network and downloads the AI model from Hugging Face. Like any web request, these services may receive technical information such as your IP address. No audio or personal content is sent. The page itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Your content

Use only music you own or have permission to use. You are responsible for how you use the files you export.
