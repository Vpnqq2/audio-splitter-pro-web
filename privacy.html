Privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Audio Splitter Pro processes your music on your own device, inside your browser. The songs you select and the tracks created are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We have no accounts and no database of users.

Data stored on your device

The AI model (about 165 MB) is saved in your browser's storage after the first download, so it does not have to be downloaded again. Your choice about advertising cookies is saved in your browser's local storage. You can remove all of this by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Third-party services

To work, the page loads the ONNX Runtime Web library from the jsDelivr content network and downloads the AI model from Hugging Face. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as with any web request. No audio or personal content is sent.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display ads from Google AdSense on its information pages (guides, about and similar). The splitter screen itself does not show ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising.
  • More about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • If you decline in our banner, only non-personalised ads are requested. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message where it is enabled.

We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to access or delete. For data held by the third parties above, please contact them directly.

Changes
