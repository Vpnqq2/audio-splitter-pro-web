Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

At AudioSplitAI, we respect your privacy. Our audio separation tool is designed to process your selected audio directly in your browser. Audio files and generated stems are not uploaded to AudioSplitAI servers.

1. Audio Processing

AudioSplitAI uses browser-based AI technology to separate audio into vocals, drums, bass and other stems. Audio processing takes place locally on your device using your browser's available resources.

We do not operate an audio upload service or store users' audio files on our servers. Your selected audio files and the resulting tracks remain on your device, unless you independently choose to share or upload them elsewhere.

2. Browser Storage

To provide the audio separation service, AudioSplitAI downloads an AI model and supporting files. The AI model (approximately 165 MB) may be stored in your browser's cache or other browser storage so that it does not need to be downloaded again on every visit.

Your advertising preferences may also be saved in your browser's local storage. You can clear stored website data, cached files and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing this data may require the AI model to be downloaded again.

3. Third-Party Services and Hosting

AudioSplitAI uses third-party services to deliver and operate the website. These services may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information and requested resources when your browser connects to them.

  • Cloudflare Pages: Hosts and delivers our website and may process technical request information for website delivery, security and reliability.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: Helps us understand website traffic and performance. The analytics service may process technical usage information to provide aggregated website statistics.
  • jsDelivr: Delivers the ONNX Runtime Web library used by the audio processing tool.
  • Hugging Face: Hosts the AI model files downloaded by your browser for audio processing.

These third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms. AudioSplitAI does not send your selected audio files or generated tracks to these providers as part of the normal browser-based audio separation process.

4. Advertising and Cookies

AudioSplitAI may display advertisements on informational pages, such as guides and other content pages, through Google AdSense or other advertising partners if advertising is enabled on the website. The audio splitter tool itself is not intended to display advertisements.

If advertising is enabled, third-party advertising providers, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Limit how often an advertisement is shown.
  • Provide personalized or non-personalized advertising, depending on your consent and applicable settings.

Where required by applicable law, visitors may be asked to provide or manage their consent before certain advertising cookies or technologies are used.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about advertising choices at aboutads.info.

For more information about how Google uses information from websites and apps that use its services, visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Cookies and Your Choices

Essential browser storage may be used to remember website preferences and support the operation of the audio tool. Third-party services may also use their own cookies or similar technologies, subject to their settings and applicable consent requirements.

You can control or delete cookies and website data through your browser settings. Blocking certain storage technologies may affect some website features.

6. Children's Privacy

AudioSplitAI is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

7. Data Security and Retention

Because audio processing takes place in your browser and we do not operate an audio storage service, we do not retain users' selected audio files or generated stems on AudioSplitAI servers.

Technical information processed by our hosting, analytics and other third-party providers may be retained according to their respective policies and retention practices.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion of information held by a service provider.

For questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy-related requests concerning AudioSplitAI, contact us using the email address below. For information held directly by third-party providers, you may also need to contact those providers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or applicable requirements. Any updates will be published on this page, and the date at the top will indicate when the policy was last updated.
