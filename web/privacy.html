Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

At AudioSplitAI, we respect your privacy. Our website and Android application are designed to process audio locally on your device. Audio files selected for normal audio separation are not uploaded to AudioSplitAI servers.

1. Audio Processing

AudioSplitAI uses AI technology to separate audio into vocals, drums, bass and other stems. On the website, audio processing takes place locally in your browser using your device's available resources.

The Android application also processes selected audio files locally on your device. Audio files and generated tracks are not uploaded to AudioSplitAI servers as part of the normal audio separation process.

We do not operate an audio upload service or store users' selected audio files or generated stems on AudioSplitAI servers. Your audio files remain on your device unless you independently choose to share or upload them elsewhere.

2. Website Browser Storage

To provide the website audio separation service, AudioSplitAI downloads an AI model and supporting files. The AI model (approximately 165 MB) may be stored in your browser's cache or other browser storage so that it does not need to be downloaded again on every visit.

Your website preferences may also be saved in your browser's local storage. You can clear stored website data, cached files and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing this data may require the AI model to be downloaded again.

3. Third-Party Services and Website Hosting

AudioSplitAI uses third-party services to deliver and operate the website. When your browser connects to these services, they may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information and requested resources.

  • Cloudflare Pages: Hosts and delivers our website and may process technical request information for website delivery, security and reliability.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: Helps us understand website traffic and performance. The analytics service may process technical usage information to provide aggregated website statistics.
  • jsDelivr: Delivers the ONNX Runtime Web library used by the website audio processing tool.
  • Hugging Face: Hosts AI model files downloaded by your browser for website audio processing.

These providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms. AudioSplitAI does not send your selected audio files or generated tracks to these providers as part of the normal browser-based audio separation process.

The services listed in this section relate to the website. They should not be understood as services used by the Android application unless specifically stated in the Android app sections below.

4. Android App: Advertising and Third-Party Services

The AudioSplitAI Android application uses third-party services to support advertising, crash reporting and push notifications. These services are provided by Google and may process certain technical information as described below.

4.1 Google AdMob

Our Android application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information, which may include advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, app information, approximate location derived from IP address, and information about ad views and interactions.

This information may be used to deliver, personalize where permitted, measure and improve advertisements, prevent fraud and provide advertising services.

The type of advertising shown may depend on your consent, location, applicable laws, device settings and advertising preferences. You can manage certain advertising preferences through your Android device settings and Google's advertising controls.

For more information, please review: Google Privacy Policy.

4.2 Firebase Crashlytics

Our Android application uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect, report and analyze application crashes and technical errors.

Crash reports may contain technical information such as device model, operating system version, application version, crash logs, stack traces, diagnostic information and identifiers used by the service to associate crash reports with an application installation.

We use this information to identify technical problems, improve application stability and maintain the quality of the app. Crash reporting information is handled by Firebase according to Google's applicable privacy and retention practices.

4.3 Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

Our Android application uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications.

To provide this service, Firebase may process information such as device registration tokens, app and device information, and technical message delivery data.

You can manage or disable notifications through your Android device settings, where supported.

For more information about Firebase data practices, please visit: Firebase Privacy and Security.

4.4 Separation of App Services and Audio Processing

The advertising, crash reporting and notification services described above support separate app functions. They are not used by AudioSplitAI to upload users' selected audio files as part of the normal local audio separation process.

Google and Firebase may independently process technical information under their own privacy policies. AudioSplitAI does not control all data processing performed by these third-party providers.

5. Website Advertising and Cookies

AudioSplitAI may display advertisements on informational website pages, such as guides and other content pages, through Google AdSense or other advertising partners if advertising is enabled on the website. The website audio splitter tool itself is not intended to display advertisements.

If advertising is enabled, third-party advertising providers, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Limit how often an advertisement is shown.
  • Provide personalized or non-personalized advertising, depending on your consent and applicable settings.

Where required by applicable law, visitors may be asked to provide or manage their consent before certain advertising cookies or technologies are used.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about advertising choices at aboutads.info.

For more information about how Google uses information from websites and apps that use its services, visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. Cookies and Your Choices

Essential browser storage may be used to remember website preferences and support the operation of the audio tool. Third-party website services may also use their own cookies or similar technologies, subject to their settings and applicable consent requirements.

You can control or delete cookies and website data through your browser settings. Blocking certain storage technologies may affect some website features.

The Android application may use technical identifiers and storage required by its integrated advertising, crash reporting and notification services. These are separate from the website's browser cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

AudioSplitAI is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

8. Data Security and Retention

Because audio processing takes place locally on your device, we do not retain users' selected audio files or generated stems on AudioSplitAI servers as part of the normal audio separation functionality.

Technical information processed by our website hosting, analytics, advertising, crash reporting and notification providers may be retained according to their respective privacy policies and retention practices.

We do not control the retention periods applied independently by Google, Firebase, Cloudflare or other third-party providers.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have rights concerning your personal information. These rights may include requesting access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, and objecting to or restricting certain processing activities.

For privacy-related requests concerning information handled directly by AudioSplitAI, contact us using the email address below.

For information processed directly by Google, Firebase, Cloudflare or other third-party providers, you may need to exercise your privacy rights through the relevant provider.

10. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Our services rely on third-party providers. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We encourage you to review the relevant policies for further information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, application, third-party services or applicable requirements. Any updates will be published on this page, and the date at the top will indicate when the policy was last updated.
