Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The website is free to use.

Is my music uploaded?

No. Everything runs inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Why does the first run take longer?

The AI engine (about 165 MB) is downloaded once and then stored in your browser.

Is it slow on my phone?

Browsers on phones have limited memory and speed. A computer works best here, and the Android app is made for phones.

Tip: reload this page once if splitting feels slow, so the browser can use more CPU threads.
